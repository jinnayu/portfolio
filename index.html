How I tend to work

I notice things.
Then I usually need to know why.

I studied Sociology before design, so I naturally start with behavior: what people are trying to do, where they hesitate, and what the system is asking them to understand.

From there, I organize the mess — into a clearer product, a stronger system, or an experiment that tells us what to do next.
