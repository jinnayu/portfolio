How I got here

Sociology first.
Design followed.

I’m a UX/UI & Product Designer with 5 years of experience in med-tech, hospitality, and health & wellness, based in Austin, Texas.

I studied Sociology at UT Austin before moving into UX/UI design. That background shaped my curiosity about how people interact with technology and experiences, and ultimately how those interactions impact society.

My favorite part of being a product designer is finding the patterns and organizing the information into a clear system people can actually use.
