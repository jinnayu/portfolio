About

Sociology background. Designer's eye. Soccer player's discipline.

Drop image: about-portrait.jpg

I came to design through a Sociology degree, not a design program — which means I start from how people actually behave, not just how interfaces are supposed to look. That background, plus a UX/UI certificate from UT Austin's School of Information, shows up in how I approach research and problem framing.

Right now I split time between contract work and an independent practice, Studio Jinner, while looking for the right in-house team to join full-time — ideally one with a design system worth sinking my teeth into.
