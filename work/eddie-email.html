04 · Lifecycle UX · Email

Eddie
Email Flow

Trust-first lifecycle design with one clear next step per message.

Role
UX/UI & lifecycle designer
Focus
Flows · campaigns · Klaviyo
Scale
100+ emails
Outcome
$30K+ in first 30 days

Challenge

Too many messages were doing too much.

Education, promotion, brand storytelling, and multiple CTAs competed inside the same email.

Approach

One email. One job.

I simplified hierarchy around three questions: Why am I getting this? What do I need to know? What should I do next? Reusable modules kept the flow visually connected to the product experience.

Impact

Clearer UX with business results attached.
