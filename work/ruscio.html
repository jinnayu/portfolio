Goal

Challenge

A team page is really a trust page.

Patients needed to understand who does what — without scanning an endless roster.

Approach

Organize by role, not roster.

I grouped the team around patient-facing roles, clarified hierarchy, and explored tabbed navigation so the page could grow without becoming longer or harder to scan.

Outcome

Clearer today. Easier to scale tomorrow.
