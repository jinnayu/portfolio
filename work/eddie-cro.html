Challenge

Traffic wasn’t the problem. Guessing was.

Instead of redesigning the funnel all at once, I broke it into testable questions around imagery, color, CTA treatment, and page context.

Approach

Test the question, not the decoration.

Each result informed the next test. The most useful finding: a similar change won on one page and lost on another. Context mattered more than a universal “best” design.

Impact

Small tests. Big lifts.
