Website Redesign

A clearer, more confident path to purchase for a sensitive health product.

Role
Lead UX/UI Designer
Focus

Platform
Shopify
Impact
129% engagement · 28% traffic

Challenge

The product was useful. The site wasn’t explaining why.

Clinical language and unclear sizing created friction. The redesign focused on two jobs: explain the product quickly and reduce uncertainty before purchase.

Approach

Tell the story, then answer the doubt.

I restructured the journey from problem → product → proof → purchase, then rebuilt sizing around visual guidance, how-to content, and video.

Impact

A stronger experience — and a better baseline.
