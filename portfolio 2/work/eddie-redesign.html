The problem

The existing site buried the product's value under generic med-tech visual language — clinical, cold, and forgettable. Nothing on the page reflected what actually made the product different.

What I did

I led the redesign end to end: research, information architecture, and final UI. That included the parallax creative direction that became the site's signature moment — a scroll-driven sequence inspired by Apple's product storytelling, built early in the project alongside the founding design team.
